# SpainPorFavor — Backlink & Directory Submission Strategy

This document outlines the actionable strategy for building backlinks from expat directories, immigration forums, and digital nomad communities to increase domain authority and AI citation likelihood.

## Why Backlinks Matter for AI Search (GEO)

AI models (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini) prioritize sources that are:
1. **Frequently cited** by other authoritative websites
2. **Listed in directories** that the AI training data includes
3. **Referenced in forums** where real users discuss the topic
4. **Structured clearly** with factual, up-to-date information

Backlinks signal authority to both traditional search engines AND AI models that crawl the web.
