## Slide 3: The Problem — A broken, opaque, high-stakes process

**Heading:** 42% of DIY visa applications are rejected on first submission

**Key points:**

- The Spanish immigration system requires 15-28 documents, apostilles, translations, and consulate appointments — with zero margin for error
- DIY applicants face a 42% rejection rate for Digital Nomad Visas (2,918 applications tracked by Nomads Embassy, 2024)
- A single rejection costs applicants 3-6 months of delay, €200+ in refiling fees, and often missed life plans (lease start dates, school enrollments, job start dates)
- Information is fragmented across government sites in Spanish, contradictory blog posts, and expensive lawyer consultations (€700-€2,500)
- The emotional cost is enormous: applicants describe the process as "Kafkaesque," "anxiety-inducing," and "the most stressful thing I've done"
